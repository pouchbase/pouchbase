---
'@pouchbasejs/plugin-sdk': minor
---

Build a plugin from one `definePlugin({ ... })` in `plugin.ts` that declares its settings, field types, pages (with their title, sidebar link, icon and `layout`: the reading width, the full width, or all of the space), sign-in providers and routes by id, importing their code, components and `.svg` icons from any folder; the CLI writes the manifest itself (`pouchbase-plugin manifest` prints it). `add` creates the files of a contribution and adds it to `plugin.ts`, `validate` checks imports and compiles components exactly as Pouchbase does, components and icons are typed (`@pouchbasejs/plugin-sdk/env`), field types, pages and sign-in providers can have icons, `@pouchbasejs/plugin-sdk/vite` lets tests import `plugin.ts`, and `@pouchbasejs/plugin-sdk/testing` provides `fakeContext()`. The former layout, with `contributes` and `server` in `pouchbase-plugin.json` and components in `ui/`, is gone: the CLI refuses those keys. `HOST_MODULES` and `isHostModule` are removed in favor of `HOST_MODULE_IDS` and `isHostModuleId`.
